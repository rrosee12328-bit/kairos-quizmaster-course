# Kairos creative brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

User: “Use your judgment and proposed direction”. Original intent: “make this site stand out”.

## Eight creative decisions
1. Vibe: cinematic, confident, professional. Burgundy identity and existing brand photography are repository evidence; direction is authored.
2. Journey: meet the academy, understand its approach, choose a training path, understand completion requirements, resolve questions, continue to the selected course.
3. Energy: bold opening, quiet introduction, interactive peak, calm practical explanation, decisive close.
4. Feeling: confidence from a framed officer portrait; trust from the academy introduction; agency from selecting a career path; clarity from the training steps; reassurance from FAQs; readiness from a specific next action.
5. Signature: choosing a security role assembles its training route and carries the selected course into the closing action and checkout destination.
6. Range: bold professional editorial, retaining burgundy rather than introducing unrelated neon styling.
7. Distinct scenes, as explicitly accepted in the proposed direction.
8. Existing logo, officer and course photography, and welcome video. No generated assets or purchased media needed.

## Purpose
Audience: prospective security trainees and returning students, evidenced by existing course and account routes.
Belief: I can understand my next training step at Kairos.
Action: Explore courses; selected-course actions say View this course.

## Grammar and fingerprint gate
Custom grammar: training field guide. Anchored chapter navigation; dimensional framed portrait opening; a role selector as the largest content section; practical steps; existing FAQ library; a selected-route closing plate. Bans: scroll hijacking, long empty pinning, autoplay, fake metrics, fake operational dashboards, repeated feature-card grids. The eight stock grammars impose structures that do not fit an existing educational enrollment journey.
Registry inspected: empty; first build passes without comparison rows.

## Feeling curve and peak
- Opening: confidence, oversized clear promise with framed professional photography.
- Introduction: trust, calm prose and user-initiated academy film.
- Path selector: agency, a chosen role resolves into its own image and route.
- Method: clarity, the online and in-person distinction is visible.
- FAQs: reassurance, existing answers stay accessible.
- Close: readiness, the chosen path persists into the action.

Peak, selector: “I picked the role I wanted and the site laid out my training path.”
Tell-someone sentence: “It's the site where choosing your security role builds your next training step.”
No authored dead scroll or dwell.

## Layer contract
Far ground: pale neutral and outlined academy lettering, smallest motion. Midground: burgundy architectural plane, moderate motion. Subject: existing complete officer photograph within an independent rectangular frame, slow translation. Foreground: frame rule/caption, opposite restrained travel. Copy and actions remain stable on a clean ground. This is a photographic editorial composition, not an extracted person; no duplicate person or invented background plate.
Mobile: copy above portrait, compact framing, no horizontal travel. Reduced motion: all content and selected routes remain usable with static layers.

## Integration
Preserve React routing, Supabase session handling, existing FAQs, existing checkout and course access behavior. Use component-local motion with cleanup: the supplied engine has global listeners and no unmount API, so mounting it on a React route would leak work on navigation. Unmodified engine copies retained here as skill reference only.
Preflight: Node and Chrome available; ffmpeg absent (no video encoding needed); generation key absent (no generation needed); Playwright available in bundled runtime, outside the preflight's resolution path.
